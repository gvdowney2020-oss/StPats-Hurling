Tendon Nutrition & Recovery Protocols
Keith Baar Protocol Tendon Collagen Primer

Take 15g hydrolysed collagen peptides (or gelatin) with 50mg Vitamin C (orange juice) roughly 45–60 minutes before training. The muscle pump forces circulating amino acids into avascular tendons.

Recovery Shield Targeted Anti-Inflammatory Nutrition

Aim for 30–35g high-leucine protein per feeding across 4 daily pulses, 2–3g Omega-3s daily for joint synovitis, and 30ml tart cherry concentrate on training nights to accelerate 48-hour recovery.
