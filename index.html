Hurler Fatigue & Overload Diagnostic Guide

Observable movement and skill breakdowns during pitch running and gym lifts.

1 Pitch Running & Skill Cues
Pitch Floor
  • Striking Dropping Short: Collapsing into wrist-only flicks; clearances falling 10m short due to hip fatigue.
  • Sluggish Deceleration: Taking 6–8 strides to brake instead of 3; sliding past breaking balls.
  • Fumbled Pickups: Needing 2 attempts to jab or roll-lift on the sprint.
2 Weight Room & Tissue Cues
Weight Room
  • Nordic Hamstring Collapse: Inability to resist for 2 seconds; dropping immediately to floor.
  • Barbell Grinding: Bar speed slowing noticeably on warm-up sets (65–75% 1RM).
  • Copenhagen Sag: Lower hip dropping toward the floor during 20s adductor holds.
