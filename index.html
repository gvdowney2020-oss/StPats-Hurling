Targeted Tendon Collagen Protocol

Keith Baar Protocol

Tendons have minimal vascular blood supply. Remodeling is driven by muscular mechanical pump contractions during warm-ups.

Pre-Gym Recipe (45–60 mins prior):
• 15–20g Hydrolysed Collagen Peptides (or Beef Gelatin)
• 50mg Vitamin C (or 150ml Orange Juice)

Directly elevates proline and hydroxyproline in the bloodstream, doubling collagen synthesis across hamstrings and adductors.

Veteran Shield Fueling (Ages 32–40)

Longevity
  • Anabolic Resistance: Veteran players require 35–40g protein per meal (vs 25g for 20-year-olds) to trigger identical muscle protein synthesis.
  • Omega-3 EPA/DHA (2–3g Daily): Lowers baseline joint synovitis and reduces lingering DOMS after hard pitch running.
  • Tart Cherry Juice (30ml Night of Session): Accelerates neuromuscular force recovery and blunts oxidative muscle damage before morning commute.
