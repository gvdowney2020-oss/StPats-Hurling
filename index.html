Hurler Fatigue & Overload Diagnostic Guide

Observable movement and skill breakdowns during pitch drills and weight room lifts.

1 Pitch & Training Cues
Field Floor
  • Striking Dropped Short: Mechanics collapsing into wrists only; clearance strikes dropping 5–10m short under zero pressure.
  • Fumbled Pickups: Missing the first jab/roll lift or requiring two attempts to secure the sliotar on the run.
  • Over-Running the Ball: Inability to brake eccentrically over 3–5 strides; sliding past the ball and conceding cheap frees.
  • Flat-Footed Aerial Duels: Jumping off one foot with minimal height; bracing for contact rather than attacking the sliotar.
2 Gym & Weight Room Cues
Weight Room
  • Concentric Bar Slowdown: Warm-up sets (60–70% 1RM) moving visibly slow or hitching on Back Squats/Deadlifts.
  • Nordic Hamstring Collapse: Inability to brake descent for even 2 seconds; immediately folding at waist and falling to hands.
  • Copenhagen Sag: Lower hip dropping toward floor during adductor holds; inability to sustain a straight lateral plank.
  • SkiErg Split Degradation: 500m split time dropping by >8–10 seconds on intervals with high perceived effort.
